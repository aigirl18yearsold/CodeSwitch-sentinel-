
# CodeSwitch Sentinel

## Research Track
AI + Languages

## Research Question
Can a character-level machine-learning
classifier distinguish English, Bangla,
Romanized Bangla, and mixed
Bangla-English messages?

## Language Categories
- EN: English
- BN: Bangla script
- BN-ROM: Romanized Bangla
- MIXED: Bangla-English mixture

## Planned Method
- Character-level TF-IDF
- Logistic Regression
- Baseline comparison
- Held-out testing
- Per-category performance analysis

## Research Integrity
- Record dataset sources.
- Avoid duplicate data leakage.
- Verify labels where possible.
- Report limitations honestly.
