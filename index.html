About This Project

LinguaLearn combines beginner-friendly English learning with CodeSwitch Sentinel, a multilingual text classification research prototype.

The learning lessons and language classifier are separate features. The classifier is an experimental model and can make incorrect predictions.

Our mission: Make language learning and multilingual communication more accessible.
